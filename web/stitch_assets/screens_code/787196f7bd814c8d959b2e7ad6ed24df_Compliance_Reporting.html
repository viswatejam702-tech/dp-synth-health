Compliance Note

The DP-Synth platform utilizes formal Differential Privacy (DP) mechanisms. By establishing a strict epsilon (ε) budget, the mathematical guarantee ensures that the presence or absence of any individual in the training dataset cannot be determined with statistical confidence, satisfying the anonymization requirements for most global frameworks including the DPDP Act.
